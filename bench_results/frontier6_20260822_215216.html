Action Tokenization: Discrete vs. Continuous Generation ### 1.1 Discrete Tokenization (RT-2, OpenVLA, RT-1) The RT-2 paradigm (Brohan et al., 2023) treats robot actions as a language: a 7-DoF end-effector pose $(x, y, z, r, p, y, g)$ is quantized into $B$ bins per dimension (typically $B=256$), yielding a vocabulary of $B^7$ possible action tokens.
